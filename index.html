RESEARCH / SUSTAINABILITY

Studying BYD
and sustainability

01

A factory in 1760

NewBiz business simulation · Regional first place

+

In the NewBiz challenge, our team ran a virtual factory set during the Industrial Revolution. We bought materials, upgraded equipment, planned production, and set prices. As the market changed, we had to adjust our decisions. We came first in our region and qualified for the national finals.

02

Learning in Hong Kong

Hong Kong programs · Business & financial decision-making

+

In Hong Kong, I studied topics including supply and demand, game theory, and regression modelling. I led teams, visited companies, and took part in simulated trading. These activities gave me a chance to try out what I had learned.

03

China Merchants Bank / Postal Savings Bank of China

+

As a lobby assistant at China Merchants Bank and Postal Savings Bank of China, I helped guide customers and learned what different staff members do. I also learned about savings, financial products, and services for local communities.
